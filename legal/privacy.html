3. Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Hosting): hosts accounts, saves, leaderboards and the game. [CHECK: the region your Firestore database is in.]

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK's adequacy regulations or by the International Data Transfer Agreement / standard contractual clauses that these providers use.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, save and wallet: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Leaderboard entries: until you leave the leaderboards or delete your account.
  • Records of sales: 6 years, as UK tax law requires, even after an account is deleted.
  • Hosting logs: as long as our providers keep them, usually days to weeks.
